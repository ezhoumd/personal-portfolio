David

2023–24 · FTC CENTERSTAGE · Team 6417 Blu Cru

A robot designed around reach and dexterity: a turret with over 360° of rotation, coaxial belt-driven linear rails mounted on a pivot, and a differential claw for grabbing game pieces.

  • Turret rotates beyond a full 360°
  • Coaxial belt-driven linear rails mounted on a pivot
  • Differential claw for grabbing game pieces
  • 10 total degrees of freedom, 6 of them in the arm
